This one went everywhere. Google rolled out AI Overviews to American users in May 2024, and within days the internet was flooded with screenshots of it confidently recommending glue as a pizza ingredient. The source? A joke post on Reddit from 2013. The AI had no concept of sarcasm and treated it like culinary wisdom. Google's response was to quietly remove AI Overviews from certain queries while insisting everything was fine. [Reported by Silicon Republic](https://www.siliconrepublic.com/machines/google-ai-overview-hallucinations-glue-pizza), [Live Science](https://www.livescience.com/technology/artificial-intelligence/googles-ai-tells-users-to-add-glue-to-their-pizza-eat-rocks-and-make-chlorine-gas), and others.
